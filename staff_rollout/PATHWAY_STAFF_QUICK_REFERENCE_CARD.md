# PATHWAY STAFF PORTAL
# Quick Reference Card

## Daily Workflow

1. Log in
2. Open `Dashboard`
3. Select an assigned client
4. Complete notes or uploads
5. Review missing items
6. Log out

## Therapist

- Use assigned clients only
- Complete SOAP notes
- Complete treatment plans
- Complete summary reports
- Complete safety plans
- Upload documents and worksheets

## Support Staff

- Use assigned clients only
- Upload documents
- Upload worksheets
- Flag missing forms
- Do not complete clinical notes

## Before Saving

- Confirm correct client
- Confirm date
- Review required fields
- Save
- Reopen if needed to confirm it saved correctly

## Document Uploads

- Open the correct client first
- Choose the correct document type
- Confirm the file appears in the client record

## Do Not

- Do not access unassigned clients
- Do not share logins
- Do not guess if something fails

## If There Is A Problem

Contact: `Dr. Barnett / Admin`

Provide:

- your name
- client name
- what you were trying to do

Authorized staff use only.
