# PATHWAY GUIDANCE SERVICES
# Staff Portal – Quick Start SOP

## Access

1. Go to the staff portal link provided
2. Log in using your assigned username and password
3. Change your password if instructed

## Your Role

You have been assigned one of the following roles:

### Therapist

- Work only with your assigned clients
- Complete SOAP notes, treatment plans, summary reports, and safety plans
- Upload documents and worksheets

### Support Staff

- Work only with assigned clients
- Upload documents and worksheets
- Flag missing forms
- Do not complete clinical notes

## Working With Clients

1. Open `Dashboard`
2. Click your assigned client
3. Select the task:

- SOAP Note
- Treatment Plan
- Summary Report
- Safety Plan
- Document Upload

## Creating Notes

- Enter all required fields
- Confirm client name and date
- Click `Save`
- Verify the note saved correctly

## Uploading Documents

- Select correct client
- Upload file (PDF, worksheet, form)
- Confirm file appears in client record

## Compliance Expectations

- Complete documentation the same day or as assigned
- Do not access clients not assigned to you
- Do not share login credentials
- Report missing forms or issues immediately

## Security Rules

- Log out when finished
- Do not use shared accounts
- Only access what is required for your role

## If Something Goes Wrong

Contact: `Dr. Barnett / Admin`

Provide:

- your name
- client name
- what you were trying to do

## Daily Workflow

1. Log in
2. Review assigned clients
3. Complete notes or uploads
4. Check for missing items
5. Log out

This system is for authorized staff use only.
