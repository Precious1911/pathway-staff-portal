# PATHWAY STAFF PORTAL – 5 MINUTE TRAINING SCRIPT

Alright team, I’m going to walk you through how we’re using our new system. This is how we will manage client documentation moving forward.

First, everyone will have their own login. Do not share your login with anyone. Once you log in, you will only see the clients assigned to you. That is intentional for privacy and compliance.

When you enter the system, you’ll land on your dashboard. From there, click on a client’s name to open their profile.

If you are a therapist, your main responsibilities are completing documentation. That includes SOAP notes, treatment plans, summary reports, and safety plans. When you open a form, fill it out completely, double check the client name and date, and then save it. Always confirm that it saved correctly before moving on.

If you are support staff, your role is different. You will not complete clinical notes. Your responsibilities are uploading documents, organizing files, and flagging anything that is missing. If you notice a missing treatment plan or intake, you flag it instead of trying to complete it.

Everyone should only work within the clients assigned to them. If you do not see a client you expect, do not try to work around the system. Let me know so I can assign access properly.

When uploading documents, always make sure you are in the correct client before uploading. Once uploaded, confirm the file appears and opens.

A few important rules. Do not access clients that are not assigned to you. Do not share login information. Always log out when you are done, especially if you are on a shared or public computer.

If something goes wrong, do not guess. Let me know what happened, which client you were working on, and what you were trying to do. We will fix it quickly.

Your daily workflow is simple. Log in, check your assigned clients, complete your documentation or uploads, review anything missing, and log out.

This system is here to keep things organized, efficient, and compliant. If you follow the process, everything will flow smoothly.

That’s it. We’ll start using it now, and I’ll be available if anyone needs help.
